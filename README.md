# 📱 [GlowUp AI]

An AI-powered mobile application that scans and analyzes facial features to provide an instant rating, along with actionable, personalized improvement routines to help you look and feel your best.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🆓 Free Tier
- **Instant Face Scan:** Quick and easy facial analysis using your phone's camera.
- **Base Rating:** Get an immediate, objective rating of your facial features.

### 💎 Premium Subscription ($4.99 / week)
Unlock your full potential with our specialized weekly plan, which includes:
- **Custom Daily Routines:** Step-by-step guides tailored to your specific facial structure and rating.
- **Product Recommendations:** Exact "things to use" (skincare, grooming, or styling products) that work for you.
- **Habit Corrections:** A clear list of "things not to do" that might be negatively impacting your appearance.
- **Progress Tracking:** Monitor your improvements over time.

---

## ⚙️ How to Install (Android)

Since this app is distributed directly and not through the Google Play Store, you may need to allow installations from unknown sources:

1. Open the downloaded `.apk` file.
2. If prompted, go to **Settings** > **Allow from this source** (or "Install unknown apps").
3. Tap **Install** and open the app!
